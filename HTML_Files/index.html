Welcome!

Welcome to EWallet! Here you can see a list of page features:

  • Income

    • Display your total amount of money
    • Show your income per month and year
    • Add/Modify/Delete income entries

  • Expenses

    • Display your total amount of money
    • Show your spending per month and year
    • Add/Modify/Delete expense entries

  • Planning

    • Display your total amount of planned purchases
    • Show how much you have added to savings per month
    • Show how long you have to meet your goal
    • Add/Modify/Delete planned purchase entries

  • Summary

    • Display a summary of your financial status
    • Show your income, expenses, and balances
    • Provide insights into your financial habits
